Welcome to Aero at Baylor

We are a student organization at Baylor University dedicated to the advancement of aerospace education and innovation. Our team participates in exciting projects, such as aircraft design, propulsion systems, and more!

About Us

Aero at Baylor is composed of passionate students, faculty mentors, and industry professionals working together to foster a collaborative environment for learning and innovation in the aerospace field. We strive to provide students with the opportunity to engage in hands-on aerospace projects, develop technical skills, and make meaningful contributions to the aerospace industry.
